No one on this floor is human.
Ten robots. Ten different jobs.
This one takes crates off the line, and stacks them.
This one loads the milling machine.
This one runs the press, with both hands on the buttons.
This one stocks the shelves.
This one checks every part.
And this one feeds a robot arm.
That arm is bolted in place. The humanoid can walk to the next job.
Why a human shape? Because factories are built for people: the buttons, the doors, the shelves. A robot shaped like us can use them without rebuilding the factory.
Car makers are already testing this. BMW has trialled a humanoid placing sheet-metal parts.
Same factory. New workers.
