The biggest airliner ever built. And Airbus stopped making it.
The A380: two full decks, four engines, over five hundred seats.
Next to a Boeing 777, it's actually a metre shorter. But its wings are fifteen metres wider.
Airbus bet on giant jets flying between huge hub airports.
Instead, airlines flew direct, on efficient twin-engine jets.
Four engines burn more fuel and need more maintenance. And five hundred seats are hard to fill every day.
Airbus pulled the plug in 2019. The last of two hundred and fifty-one was delivered in 2021. Emirates took almost half.
Too big for the way we fly.
